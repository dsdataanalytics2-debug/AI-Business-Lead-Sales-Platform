# Decisions
- [YYYY-MM-DD] Decision — reason — alternatives rejected — who approved
- [2026-10-02] CRM Stage & Lead Assignment Permissions — Guard lead assignment and assignee directory behind dedicated `LEADS_ASSIGN` permission rather than reusing `LEADS_WRITE` — reason: sales representatives should be allowed to update stage and add notes (`LEADS_WRITE`) without being able to reassign leads across team members — alternatives rejected: single `LEADS_WRITE` role check or role hardcoding — approved during M3 Step 1 & Step 3.
- [2026-10-02] Follow-Up Tasks Audit-Only Logging — Follow-up task lifecycle events emit AuditLog entries (`lead.follow_up_*`) but do NOT create `CrmActivity` records — reason: keep customer-facing CRM interaction history focused on direct lead engagements (notes, stage transitions, assignment) rather than internal task management noise — alternatives rejected: dedicated task CRM activities — approved during M3 Step 4 & Step 5.
- [2026-10-03] Derived Follow-Up Overdue Status — Compute task overdue status dynamically in code (`status === PENDING && dueAt < now`) without persisting an `OVERDUE` database enum or running periodic cron updates — reason: eliminates background worker / scheduler dependency and prevents stale persisted states — alternatives rejected: cron-based database status updates — approved during M3 Step 5 & Step 7.
- [2026-10-03] Strict Follow-Up Terminal State Invariants — Reject cross-terminal transitions (`CANCELLED` -> complete and `COMPLETED` -> cancel) with 422 ValidationError, while treating repeat calls on the same terminal state as idempotent 200 no-ops — reason: prevents ambiguous task resurrection without an explicit reopen lifecycle — alternatives rejected: silent state overwrites or throwing 500 — approved during M3 Step 7.
- [2026-10-03] StoreMate Demo Provider Abstraction & Sales Artifact Model — Integrate StoreMate behind a pluggable `DemoWebsiteProvider` abstraction (`STOREMATE`, `MOCK`) treating demo websites as temporary, non-official sales artifacts (`isDemo: true`, `noindex: true`, `nofollow: true`) with exactly one active demo per `(organizationId, leadId)` and backend-only credentials — reason: guarantees tenant isolation, prevents search engine indexing leaks, eliminates duplicate demo sprawl, and prevents credential exposure — alternatives rejected: direct coupling to StoreMate HTTP transport in controllers, client-provided demo URLs, or multi-version demo sprawl in MVP — approved during M4 Step 1.
- [2026-10-03] StoreMate Demo Data Minimization & Contact Safety — Outbound demo payloads strictly project verified public business/contact/branding/social data while completely excluding internal CRM notes, follow-up notes, audit trails, and authentication credentials, and strictly enforce `PHONE != WHATSAPP` safety — reason: prevents internal sales intelligence leaks and ensures compliance with contact trust rules — alternatives rejected: passing unfiltered lead objects or CRM notes to StoreMate — approved during M4 Step 1.
